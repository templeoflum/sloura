## Sound

In Hedawn's words: "its my high fidilety chopepd and screwed music moniker" (S1).

### Weave

A Weave is only slowed copies, every one slowed to the same speed. Staggering them makes the chop; the original is never laid over them, since it would be off-beat (W-b02).

Production can add anything: samples, synthesized sounds and more (W-b03).

Chopped or chopless does not matter; they are just two versions (W-b04).

- Screw ran two copies of a record, one a beat behind the other (01 orientation 4).
- Every project also exported a "[Chopless Version]" (ev §2). Two covers are marked "[Chopless version]" (ev §3).
- The chop is the line Houston draws: "the chops are sacred to the culture" (Slater, 03 #1).
- "chopped not slopped" (OG Ron C, 02 #14).

### Speed

There is no set speed; it varies by feel (W-b05).

- The Weaves run 6:27, 4:46 and 4:04 (ev §2).
- No. 1 and No. 3 run about 1.41–1.42 times the length of their originals (ev §2).
- That is a rough length ratio, not a measured speed; nothing was played (ev §2).
- The 0.7× and 6-semitone figures are ev §2's arithmetic from that ratio (ev §2).

### Fidelity

"High fidelity" means extremely high-quality production: a clean source, reproduced drums, added sub, and the audio reprocessed to sound good on a festival system (W-b06).

- SoundCloud files Weave No. 1 under the genre "HiFi Screw" (ev §4).
- All three Weaves are 16-bit / 44.1 kHz AIFF (ev §2).
- The Time Moves Slow original on the Mac is 24-bit / 96 kHz (ev §2).
- Reverb: not measured, since nothing was played (ev §2).

### Source songs

So far soft indie and pop, not Houston rap (stock: Bandcamp). Sources can be anything (W-b01).

- "Time Moves Slow" by BADBADNOTGOOD feat. Samuel T. Herring, "Ocean Eyes" by Billie Eilish, "Angels" by The xx (stock: Bandcamp).
- All three Weaves are on Bandcamp as "released March 17, 2026" (stock: Bandcamp). Only No. 1 is on SoundCloud, uploaded 2025-09-04 (stock: SoundCloud).
- *Reading:* that follows Slim K screwing whole indie-R&B records (02 #13), and the soft-pop sources of Eccojams and *Floral Shoppe* (02 #22, 03 #11).
- *Reading:* it is further from Future Screw, which screwed EDM and trap (02 orientation 4, 02 #27).

### Slowed + reverb

Hedawn calls Sloura "chopepd and screwed", not slowed (S1).

- Slowed + reverb is usually dated to Slater's 2017 upload. He grew up on Screw; the big channels after him don't mention "Screw or Houston" (03 #1).
- Houston answered in Aug 2020: "Give DJ Screw his flowers. Slowed + reverb is a poor imitation of what chopped and screwed music is." (Shelby Stewart, 03 #2). Critics called it gentrification and "cultural erasure" (03 #3). Wikipedia files both "future screw" and slowed + reverb under chopped and screwed (03 #7).
- Each Weave's Bandcamp tags: "Chopped & Screwed", "Devotional", "future screw", "screw", "screw tapes", "slowed + reverb", "Minnesota" (ev §4). They sit on both sides, and use "future screw" though those edits are HeDawn's (S7).
- What marks Sloura off in public today: the bio (ev §3) and the "Weave" titles (stock: Bandcamp). So do the "Chopped & Screwed" and "HiFi Screw" labels, beside its own "slowed + reverb" tag (ev §4).
