The Weeknd’s Echoes of Silence Chopped and Screwed by Slim K

By The FADER
January 05, 2012



Slim K, a young Atlanta/Houston-based screw devotee who has been making album slow-downs since 2004 (Usher's Confessions was his first), chopped up The Weeknd's final, and arguably darkest trilogy installment, Echoes of Silence. On it, the dive of Clams Casino’s "The Fall" beat crawls slower and scarier.
