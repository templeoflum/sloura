## 2. The Future Screw Crew

What the public pages say it was (source claims, copied as shown):

- SoundCloud https://soundcloud.com/futurescrew — "A collective of Producers/DJs bringing you the best in chopped & screwed electronic music." City "TX/NY/CA/UK - INTERNET". 800 followers, 21 tracks, 9 playlists. Members listed: Trill Scott Heron, Satchmo Screws, El Blanco Nino, SubxChronic, Minimz, **HeDawn (soundcloud.com/hedawn)**, SofaKing, China Alarm. (The bio also gives a contact email; not copied here.)
- X https://twitter.com/futurescrew — "#FUTURESCREW // #LEANHOUSE // #CODEINEFOLK", the same eight names with HEDAWN, places "TX//NY//CA//MN \\ UK", joined February 2013, 432 posts, 93 followers. A guess, not checked: MN is HeDawn.
- LiveMixtapes, Purple Summer (uploaded Aug 30, 2013, 16,120 views): "In late spring, ambassador Trill Scott Herron journeyed virtually to distant stretches of the purple world and gathered a team of aspiring utopian screw heads. He made them knights of the Purple People and they named themselves the Future Screw Crew. After months of hard work, intense summer heat, and thousands of styrofoam cups, they are releasing their first group compilation of their best Future Screw edits and remixes. For more info check out: http://futurescrewcrew.tumblr.com"

## 4. Written about: the Green Label article (added T-0311)

Hedawn, answering T-0308: "there was a article on some mountain something or other page that was an offshoot of complex magazine, rpaobly only up on the wayback machine now". That fits Green Label, Mountain Dew's online magazine made with Complex Media (closed 2018). Its pages are gone from the live web; the Internet Archive has them. The article below is the only Future Screw piece in Green Label's archived pages, so it is the likely match; **not yet confirmed by Hedawn** as the one he remembers.

- **"Future Screw: The Internet's Version of Houston's Chopped and Screwed"**, Green Label (Sound), written by Ross Figlerski, posted Sun, 03.01.15 (1 March 2015). Archived copies: https://web.archive.org/web/20150304144250/http://greenlabel.com/sound/future-screw-the-internets-version-of-houstons-chopped-and-screwed/ and a later one at https://web.archive.org/web/20200711213112/http://greenlabel.com/future-screw-the-internets-version-of-houstons-chopped-and-screwed/ (both saved in `evidence/t0311/`, read 2026-10-13).
- What it says (source claims, copied as shown): Future Screw is "the chopped-and-screwed version of modern electronic dance music, with Dutch house synths, affected stutters, and a vibrant Tumblr aesthetic". It quotes **Future Screw Crew producer El Blanco Nino** by email ("It's more Internet-based … Most of us are influenced and/or involved in SoundCloud"; "as the people that started Future Screw we are bridging the gap between his work and the world of electronic music"), names **Trill Scott Heron** as the Future Screw founder whose SoundCloud ("over 120 remixes and edits made by Trill and his peers … the defining Soundcloud for the genre for a two-year period") had just been shut down for copyright, and quotes Hot Britches (#neversleep) and Slim K (via Jenesis Magazine).
- **It does not name HeDawn.** The word does not appear in either saved copy, and no SoundCloud track is embedded in the text (checked by searching the saved pages). It belongs in the stock as the press write-up of the crew and the genre HeDawn was part of, not as a piece about HeDawn himself.
- Green Label also kept a "Future Screw" tag page (archived 2015-03-05, saved); it lists only this article. Its other screw piece, "Chopped 'N' Screwed Tracks" (Caleb Pitts, 30 Sept 2015), is a general list and does not mention HeDawn or the crew (saved, checked the same way).
