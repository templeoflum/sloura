### The Future Screw Crew

What the public pages say it was (source claims):

- SoundCloud https://soundcloud.com/futurescrew: "A collective of Producers/DJs bringing you the best in chopped & screwed electronic music." City "TX/NY/CA/UK - INTERNET". Members listed: Trill Scott Heron, Satchmo Screws, El Blanco Nino, SubxChronic, Minimz, **HeDawn**, SofaKing, China Alarm. The crew's own uploads run from 2013-04-14 to 2016-03-17.
- X https://twitter.com/futurescrew: "#FUTURESCREW // #LEANHOUSE // #CODEINEFOLK", the same eight names, places "TX//NY//CA//MN \\ UK", joined February 2013.
- LiveMixtapes, Purple Summer (Aug 30, 2013): "… gathered a team of aspiring utopian screw heads. He made them knights of the Purple People and they named themselves the Future Screw Crew. …"
- Wikipedia, "Chopped and screwed", lists "future screw" as a subgenre that grew from SoundCloud producers fusing chopped and screwed with EDM in the mid-2010s. It does not name the crew.

### Written about: Green Label, 1 March 2015

**"Future Screw: The Internet's Version of Houston's Chopped and Screwed"**, Green Label (Sound), Mountain Dew's online magazine made with Complex Media, written by Ross Figlerski. Confirmed by Hedawn (T-0325) as the article he remembered. The site closed in 2018; the article survives on the Internet Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20150304144250/http://greenlabel.com/sound/future-screw-the-internets-version-of-houstons-chopped-and-screwed/ (saved copies in `evidence/t0311/`).

It describes Future Screw as "the chopped-and-screwed version of modern electronic dance music, with Dutch house synths, affected stutters, and a vibrant Tumblr aesthetic", quotes Future Screw Crew producer El Blanco Nino ("as the people that started Future Screw we are bridging the gap between his work and the world of electronic music"), and names Trill Scott Heron as the founder. **It does not name HeDawn** (checked by searching both saved copies). It is the press write-up of the crew and the genre HeDawn was part of, not a piece about him.
