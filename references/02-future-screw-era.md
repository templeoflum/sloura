# Reference library 2: Future Screw and the internet-era screw scene (2010s)

Board task T-0657. Gathered 14 Oct 2026 from public pages, no logins. Every URL below was opened in this run. The copy of each page (or the failure) is in `evidence/t0657/`, `evidence/t0657/README.md` maps each entry to its saved file, and `fetch-log.tsv` gives the HTTP result and time of each fetch. Nothing listed in `stock.md` ("Before Sloura") is repeated here. Where an entry builds on it, it says "see stock.md".

**Orientation**
1. **Who:** The Future Screw Crew (Trill Scott Heron, El Blanco Nino, Satchmo, SofaKing, Minimz, China Alarm, SubxChronic, HeDawn) and its close circle (Sir CRKS, BassBear!!), working in the wider internet-era slowed scene. That scene runs from witch house, Eccojams and cloud rap to OG Ron C's Chopstars and slowed + reverb.
2. **Where:** Online first. SoundCloud, Tumblr, YouTube, Bandcamp, Mixcloud and LiveMixtapes/ClubTapes, with members spread across "TX/NY/CA/MN/UK". Trill Scott Heron's archived profile gives "Houston/Austin".
3. **When:** The crew's Tumblr runs from 2013 (a bulk upload of older edits) to 16 Mar 2016. Its YouTube videos are from Nov–Dec 2013. The scenes around it run from 2009 (witch house, the Bristol "purple" sound) through 2011 (*Instrumentals*, *Live.Love.A$AP*) and 2012 (EDM trap) to 2017 (slowed + reverb).
4. **What made the sound:** Screw's slow-down and chops, applied to EDM, trap, Jersey/Baltimore club, future bass and indie R&B rather than Houston rap. The edits carry tags like "Future Screw Edit", "Lean House", "Jersey Lean", "Screwd N Chopd" and "Blanco $crewed It".
5. **The world around it:** The crew's whole vocabulary comes from lean (purple, drank, syrup, codeine and promethazine tag nearly every post). It ran on free downloads and remixes of other people's records. Elsewhere, the same decade argued about credit: "chopped not slopped" against sloppy slowing, and slowed + reverb against the erasure of Screw.

## C. Press and blog writing on the internet-era screw scene

12. **"7 Chopped 'N' Screwed Tracks That Shouldn't Work, But Do"** by Caleb Pitts, *Green Label*, 30 Sep 2015. *Type: article (listicle).*
    Where: Internet Archive https://web.archive.org/web/20160627112918/http://greenlabel.com/chopped-n-screwed-tracks/ (copy saved in T-0311, `evidence/t0311/`; not listed in stock.md)
    Why it matters: It came from the same magazine, six months after its Future Screw article. Screwed Miley Cyrus, Celine Dion, the Beach Boys and Chopin show that by 2015 slowing any song had become an internet habit. It quotes: "This is for advanced-intermediate screwheads only." It names Slim K's screwed Michael Jackson. [verified]

13. **"The Weeknd's Echoes of Silence Chopped and Screwed by Slim K"** by The FADER, 5 Jan 2012. *Type: blog post.*
    Where: https://www.thefader.com/2012/01/05/the-weeknds-echoes-of-silence-chopped-and-screwed-by-slim-k
    Why it matters: Slim K is the full-album slow-down DJ that Green Label also cites, described here as "a young Atlanta/Houston-based screw devotee who has been making album slow-downs since 2004". Screwing whole indie-R&B albums is the line that runs into Sloura's Weaves (XX, Billie Eilish, BADBADNOTGOOD). [verified]

14. **"Interview: OG Ron C on The Evolution of Slowed and Chopped Music"** by Reverb News (byline not in the saved copy), 9 Apr 2020. *Type: interview.*
    Where: https://reverb.com/news/og-ron-c-on-the-evolution-of-slowed-down-music (live returned 403. Internet Archive snapshot 7 Oct 2025: https://web.archive.org/web/20251007215116/https://reverb.com/news/og-ron-c-on-the-evolution-of-slowed-down-music)
    Why it matters: The source for **"chopped not slopped"**: Ron C's name for his treatment "in reference to the influx of new slow music practitioners who lack his turntable deftness". He also says: "Chopped and Screwed is DJ Screw's version of slowed down music. So, the genre is slowed down music." It covers his work on Drake's *Take Care* and Barry Jenkins's *Moonlight*. It is the craft standard that Sloura's "sonic philology" sets itself against. [verified via the Wayback copy]

15. **The Chopstars** (and **OG Ron C**) by Wikipedia contributors, current revisions read 14 Oct 2026. *Type: encyclopedia articles.*
    Where: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chopstars · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OG_Ron_C
    Why it matters: These cover the Houston-side lineage that kept turntable chopping alive through the 2010s while the Future Screw scene grew online. They list ChopNotSlop Radio (described as "the first 24 hours chopped and screwed radio station", created 2008), the ChopNotSlop *Moonlight* soundtrack (Feb 2017), and "Channel Purple" (2012) in the discography (a Frank Ocean chop by its title, which is my reading and **unverified**). [verified; the Chopstars' founding year differs between sources, so treat it as **unverified**]

16. **"How Slowed + Reverb Became The Soundtrack To Gen Z Lockdown"** by Clash Magazine (byline not found in the saved copy), 20 Apr 2021. *Type: magazine feature.*
    Where: https://www.clashmusic.com/features/how-slowed-reverb-became-the-soundtrack-to-gen-z-lockdown
    Why it matters: It traces the slowing habit forward past Future Screw, "originating in 2017", into TikTok and YouTube, and it carries the credit argument: "That influence is the late DJ Screw." Sloura will be heard next to slowed + reverb, so this is the debate it walks into. [verified]

## D. Adjacent scenes (sounds the crew drew on or ran beside)

19. **Witch house** by Wikipedia contributors, current revision. *Type: encyclopedia article.*
    Where: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witch_house
    Why it matters: Other names it lists: "drag", "screwgaze". It names DJ Screw's technique as "a foundational influence" and Salem as the pioneer. The term was coined "in December 2009" by Travis Egedy (Pictureplane) "as a joke". It is the first internet genre to screw electronic music, three years before Future Screw. [verified]

20. **"Remember Witch House?"** by Larry Fitzmaurice, *Stereogum*, 28 Sep 2020. *Type: retrospective column (Salem's *King Night* at 10).*
    Where: https://stereogum.com/2089949/witch-house-salem-king-night-turns-10/columns/sounding-board
    Why it matters: It describes the witch house sound as "slowed-down hip-hop rhythms that took direct inspiration from the syrupy sound of late Houston pioneer DJ Screw". It is a good account of how blog-era microgenres rose and fell, the same arc Future Screw followed. [verified]

21. **"The Short Life, Weird Death, and Mild Rebirth of the Witch House Genre"** by Sam Hockley-Smith, *Grantland*, 9 Jun 2015. *Type: article.*
    Where: https://grantland.com/hollywood-prospectus/the-short-life-weird-death-and-mild-rebirth-of-the-witch-house-genre/
    Why it matters: Written the same year as Green Label's Future Screw piece. It describes witch house as drawing on "the slow-and-low remixes of DJ Screw" and warns about genres that are "all surface, with one idea dragged across multiple songs". That is a useful caution for Sloura's own identity. [verified]

22. **Chuck Person's Eccojams Vol. 1** by Daniel Lopatin (as Chuck Person), The Curatorial Club, 8 Aug 2010. *Type: album.* Context: **Vaporwave** (Wikipedia).
    Where: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Person%27s_Eccojams_Vol._1 · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaporwave
    Why it matters: Wikipedia tags it "chopped and screwed": "chopped and looped samples of various songs … processed with effects such as delay, reverb, and pitch shifting; the results highlight mournful or existential moments". That is close to a definition of a Sloura Weave, made from 1980s pop instead of rap. [verified]

23. **Instrumentals** by Clams Casino, self-released 7 Mar 2011. *Type: mixtape.*
    Where: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instrumentals_(mixtape)
    Why it matters: The cloud-rap source text. SofaKing's "Clams Casino – I'm God (SofaKing Chopped & Screwed)" opens Purple Summer's SoundCloud mix, which shows the crew screwing the internet's own slow music. [verified for release facts. The crew link comes from the Tumblr track list]

    Where: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live.Love.ASAP
    Why it matters: "Purple Swag" (single, 5 Dec 2011) and the "chopped and screwed choruses" took Houston's purple sound to New York and onto the blogs just before the crew formed. The crew's YouTube screws A$AP Rocky. [verified]
