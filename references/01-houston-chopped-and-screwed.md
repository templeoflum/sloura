**Orientation**
1. **Who:** DJ Screw (Robert Earl Davis Jr., 1971–2000) and the rappers who freestyled on his tapes, the Screwed Up Click (Fat Pat, Big Hawk, Lil' Keke, E.S.G., Big Moe, Big Pokey, Lil' Flip, Z-Ro and others); on the north side, Michael "5000" Watts and Swishahouse.
2. **Where:** Houston's south side (South Park, Third Ward, Screw's house studio, the "Wood Room"), sold from his home and, from 1998, Screwed Up Records & Tapes at 7717 Cullen Blvd; Swishahouse grew out of a house in the north side's Rosewood area.
3. **When:** The first slowed tapes date from about 1990 (RBMA); the scene peaked in the mid-to-late 1990s; Screw died 16 Nov 2000; Swishahouse (founded 1997) carried the style into the 2004–05 national moment ("Still Tippin'", *Who Is Mike Jones?*).
4. **What made the sound:** Two copies of a record, one run a beat behind the other, slowed down ("screwed") with lines cut back and forth or repeated ("chopped"), plus live freestyles. Screw recorded them to cassette, mostly 100-minute gray Maxell tapes, the "gray tapes", hand-labelled and dubbed for sale.
5. **The world around it:** Slab car culture, the codeine-promethazine "lean"/"syrup" drink the music celebrated and which contributed to Screw's death (coroner, per Wikipedia), and a mixtape economy that worked outside the record industry.
